Study smarter

For students at any level. The pattern that keeps coming up from people who actually learn this way is the same: make it test you instead of summarising for you, then make it explain what you got wrong.

01

Quiz me from my own notes

Use this instead of rereading. Rereading feels like studying and is mostly not.

 → 02

Test me until I get one wrong

Use this when you think you know a topic and want to find out whether you do.

 → 03

Why did I get this wrong

Use this on a returned test or practice paper, on the questions you lost marks on.

 → 04

Let me explain it and find my holes

Use this when you can follow the material while reading but cannot reproduce it afterwards.

 → 05

Decode a paragraph I cannot get through

Use this on dense academic reading, when you have read the same three lines four times.

 → 06

Turn this into flashcards

Use this when card-making is eating the time you meant to spend studying.

 → 07

Am I actually ready for this exam

Use this a week out, when you want a real answer instead of a feeling.

 → 08

Plan the week before the exam

Use this when you know what is coming and have no idea how to fit it in.
