Best prompts for beginners

The prompts to learn first, for anyone who has heard of AI but never really used it. None of them need any setup. Copy one, fill in the brackets, paste it in.

01

Make it ask before it answers

Use this first, on anything that matters. It is the habit that separates people who get useful answers from people who get generic ones.

 → 02

Rewrite my question before you answer it

Use this when you can feel that your question is vague but you are not sure how to sharpen it.

 → 03

Answer only from what I gave you

Use this whenever the answer has to be right: a contract, a policy, a report, anything you would be embarrassed to get wrong.

 → 04

Show me what you assumed

Use this on the answer you just got, before you act on any of it.

 → 05

Explain it like I am new

Use this when someone used a word or an idea you did not follow and you would rather not pretend you did.

 → 06

Say my request back to me

Use this before a long job, when you want to catch a misunderstanding while it is still cheap.

 → 07

Break down the thing I keep avoiding

Use this on the task that has been moving down your list all week.

 → 08

Work out what to hand over

Use this in your first week, once you have a rough sense of what it can do.
