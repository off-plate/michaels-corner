Job hunting

Tailoring a CV to one specific posting is the job people reach for AI to do most often. It is also where it most often writes in things you never did. Every prompt here is built to stop that.

01

Tailor my CV to this one posting

Use this per application. It is the twenty minutes of rewriting you do for every job, done in two.

 → 02

What is this posting actually asking for

Use this before you decide whether to apply at all.

 → 03

Where am I genuinely under-qualified

Use this when you are torn about applying and want a straight answer rather than encouragement.

 → 04

Turn my duties into results

Use this when your CV reads like a job description and you know it.

 → 05

A note instead of a cover letter

Use this when a cover letter is expected but you know the usual template reads as filler.

 → 06

Read this as a tired recruiter

Use this on your finished application, before you send it.

 → 07

Rehearse the interview for this job

Use this the evening before, out loud if you can.

 → 08

Reply to silence, and to a low offer

Use this when you are staring at the message box, rewriting the first line for the fifth time.
