Founders and small business owners

For people running a small business: customers, reviews, numbers and suppliers, plus the messages you keep putting off, drafted so you can actually send them. 8 prompts, all free to copy.

01

Find the real complaint in reviews

Use this when reviews are piling up and you want the real pattern without the noise.

 → 02

Reply to an unhappy customer

Use this when a complaint lands and you want to answer calmly instead of at midnight in the wrong tone.

 → 03

Describe what I sell, grounded

Use this when you need product or service copy and want it built from real details, never invented ones.

 → 04

Announce a price increase honestly

Use this when costs moved, your prices have to follow, and you keep postponing the announcement.

 → 05

Read my weekly numbers for me

Use this when you have the numbers but never the quiet hour to see what they are saying.

 → 06

Decide what I stop doing

Use this when you are the bottleneck in your own business and every task feels like it needs you.

 → 07

Get an honest competitor read

Use this when you want a sober look at a competitor, without the anxiety and without fabricated intel.

 → 08

Prep me to negotiate

Use this when a negotiation is coming, a supplier, a landlord, a big client, and you want to walk in prepared instead of hopeful.
