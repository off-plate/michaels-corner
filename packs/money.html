Money decisions

Personal money, where a confident wrong answer costs you something real. Every prompt here forces it to work from your numbers and to show the arithmetic, because getting sums wrong in a certain voice is the thing it does most reliably.

01

Where is my money actually going

Use this when the month ends and you genuinely do not know where it went.

 → 02

Which debt do I pay first

Use this when you have several and are paying a bit off each with no plan.

 → 03

Second opinion on my plan

Use this when you have decided something and want it stress-tested before you act.

 → 04

Compare these two options properly

Use this on any either-or with numbers: two insurance plans, two loans, buy against rent, two contracts.

 → 05

What have I forgotten to count

Use this before committing to anything with an ongoing cost.

 → 06

Explain this document in plain words

Use this on an insurance policy, a contract, a statement, anything you signed without fully reading.

 → 07

Build a buffer from nothing

Use this when every unexpected bill becomes a small crisis.

 → 08

Rehearse the money conversation

Use this before talking to a partner, a bank, or someone you owe.
