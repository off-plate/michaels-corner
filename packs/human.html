Sounding like a human

People spot AI writing now, and they think less of you for sending it. These strip the tells, keep your own voice, and cut the padding that gets added when you ask for something to be made better.

01

Write in my voice, from samples

Use this instead of asking for a friendly tone. Adjectives make it guess, examples do not.

 → 02

Strip the AI tells out of this

Use this on anything generated that you are about to put your name on.

 → 03

Cut this in half

Use this on anything that felt long while you were writing it.

 → 04

Would this get read as AI, and why

Use this when something you wrote yourself has started to feel machine written to you.

 → 05

Fix my grammar and nothing else

Use this when you want the errors gone but the writing left alone.

 → 06

Same message, three readers

Use this when the same news has to go to your boss, your team and a client.

 → 07

Take the corporate out of this

Use this when your own draft has gone stiff and you cannot hear it any more.

 → 08

Titles that are not clickbait

Use this when you need a subject line, a heading or a title and every option sounds either dull or oversold.
