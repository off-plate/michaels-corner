Freelance client handling

For people who are the whole business. The recurring theme from freelancers is the conversations around the work: quoting, scope creep, silence and chasing money. These are for those.

01

The client has gone quiet

Use this five days into silence, when you are not sure if you are being ignored or forgotten.

 → 02

Talk me to a number before I lowball it

Use this before you send a price, especially if you have a habit of going in low.

 → 03

This is out of scope, said kindly

Use this on the third small favour that was never in the agreement.

 → 04

Turn their vague brief into a specification

Use this before you quote on anything described in a paragraph or less.

 → 05

Chase the invoice

Use this when payment is overdue and you have started rehearsing the message in the shower.

 → 06

The update they did not ask for

Use this mid-project, on a Friday, on anything running longer than a fortnight.

 → 07

Rehearse the price conversation

Use this before a call where you have to say a number out loud.

 → 08

Turn down the wrong project

Use this on the job you know you should not take and are talking yourself into.
