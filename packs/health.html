Health and habits

Habits, food and follow-through. These do not replace a doctor and they will not know your body, so the prompts are built to make it question you rather than prescribe at you. Anything with a symptom in it belongs in front of a professional.

01

Ten questions to find what is really stopping me

Use this when you know what you should be doing and are somehow not doing it.

 → 02

Meals from what is actually in the fridge

Use this at 6pm when you are about to order in for the third time this week.

 → 03

The smallest version of this habit

Use this when you have started the same habit four times and quit it four times.

 → 04

Sanity check something I read online

Use this on any confident health claim you saw on a video or a forum.

 → 05

Why did I stop, without the lecture

Use this after a streak breaks, instead of starting again from scratch on Monday.

 → 06

Turn a vague goal into a weekly plan

Use this when your goal is a feeling rather than something you could measure.

 → 07

Check in on me honestly

Use this weekly, in the same chat, once a plan is running.

What would need to be true for this to work

Use this before starting something big, while quitting is still cheap.
